# OneCart Family 1.3.0

## Summary

- Version **1.3.0**, iPhone only (app and widget extension). Feature release: redesign, new
  icon and widget localization since 1.2.1.
- Minimum OS: **iOS 27.0** (was iOS 26). Devices still on iOS 26 cannot install or update to
  this version.
- Design: system glass add button, progress strip as a safe-area bar, system list chrome for
  Cart and History, system button styles, tab bar that minimizes on scroll.
- Icon: check-mark cart in all four themes ([assets/brand/README.md](../../../assets/brand/README.md)).
- Widgets: English, Russian and Ukrainian strings; accented and clear Home Screen rendering;
  VoiceOver labels on the purchase toggles; correct empty-cart state.
- Stability: a crash on duplicate product IDs from CloudKit imports; a store-wipe gate that
  failed on non-English devices; account deletion that could disable sync permanently; the
  active shared cart switching on sync; lost purchase state in name dedupe; and further sync,
  persistence and notification fixes.
- Engineering: Swift 6 language mode; `REQ-<AREA>-NNN` traceability in
  [product.md](../../requirements/product.md); CI on the preview `xcode-27` runner image
  ([ADR 0003](../../decisions/0003-ci-split.md)).

## What’s New — English

- A refreshed design for iOS 27, with system glass controls and cleaner Cart and History lists.
- A new app icon, in every theme.
- Widgets now follow your language and adapt to tinted and clear Home Screens.
- Many stability and sync improvements.
- Requires iOS 27.

## What’s New — Russian

- Обновлённый дизайн для iOS 27: системные стеклянные элементы и более чистые списки корзины и
  истории.
- Новая иконка приложения во всех темах.
- Виджеты говорят на вашем языке и подстраиваются под тонированный и прозрачный экран «Домой».
- Множество улучшений стабильности и синхронизации.
- Требуется iOS 27.
