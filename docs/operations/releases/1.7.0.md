# OneCart Family 1.7.0

## Summary

- Version **1.7.0**, iPhone only (app and widget extension). Feature release after
  [1.6.0](1.6.0.md).
- Shopping trip as a Live Activity: «Я в магазине» in the cart's progress header puts the cart
  on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with progress, the first three lines to buy
  with a check control, and a stop control (REQ-WIDGET-040…060).
- Siri and Shortcuts: add items, read what is left, and start the trip by voice, in English,
  Russian and Ukrainian (REQ-SIRI-010…040).
- History day detail shows who added each item next to who bought it (REQ-SYNC-020); new
  optional attribute `HistoryItem.createdByName`, CloudKit field
  `CD_HistoryItem.CD_createdByName`.
- Invite share sheet is presented from the Share cart row (a popover on a wide scene, the
  same sheet as before on a normal iPhone); the launch ride is centred on its own view with no
  fixed device size.
- Live Activity behavior: Stop dismisses a finished trip at once; sign-out, deletion or a
  revoked login end a trip even while it is starting, and a failed startup keeps it; a
  relaunch adopts only the trip of the signed-in account and cart; a Lock Screen check updates
  the card before returning; a swiped-away card is noticed; the card goes stale an hour after
  its last change; refusals name their cause; trip progress is labelled for VoiceOver, the
  stop control is 44 × 44 pt and the check control 44 × 30 pt (three rows within the 160 pt
  card height).
- Siri behavior: a failed start is reported and retried instead of "sign in first"; requests
  are answered within 10 s, wait for a cart still being set up, report a partial add, split
  dictated lists on the word for "and" (Russian, Ukrainian, English), read items in the cart
  screen's order, and speak in the request's language with plural-aware counts.
- On-device diagnostics: MetricKit metric and diagnostic reports are written to the unified
  log (category `metrics`) through `MetricManager`; nothing leaves the device.
- Engineering: a UI smoke suite (`OneCartUITests`, demo mode) runs in every `Tests` run;
  CloudKit participant decisions are unit-tested through a seam; Sign in with Apple and the
  repository's view-context reads are main-actor isolated.
- Release prerequisite: `CD_createdByName` on `CD_HistoryItem` deployed to the CloudKit
  Production schema.
- Not covered by automated checks: the Live Activity on a Lock Screen and in the Dynamic
  Island, Siri by voice, a Lock Screen check on a cold-launched app, the stale dimming,
  "added by" across two devices, and real MetricKit reports (the simulator delivers none).

## What’s New — English

- Going shopping? Start a shopping trip from the cart: your list appears on the Lock Screen and
  in the Dynamic Island, and you can check items off right there.
- Siri can add items to your cart (say several at once: "milk, bread and eggs"), tell you what is
  left to buy, and start the shopping trip.
- History now shows who added each item as well as who bought it.

## What’s New — Russian

- Идёте в магазин? Начните поход прямо из корзины: список появится на экране блокировки и в
  Dynamic Island, и покупки можно отмечать прямо там.
- Siri может добавить товары в корзину (можно несколько сразу: «молоко, хлеб и яйца»),
  рассказать, что осталось купить, и начать поход в магазин.
- В истории теперь видно, кто добавил каждый товар и кто его купил.
