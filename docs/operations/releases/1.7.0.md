# OneCart Family 1.7.0

## Candidate

- Version/build: **1.7.0 (1)** locally for the app and widget extension, Debug and Release;
  iPhone only. Xcode Cloud assigns the uploaded build number.
- Type: feature release (MINOR) after [1.6.0](1.6.0.md). Task brief:
  [backlog-1.7.0.md](../../tasks/backlog-1.7.0.md); the Live Activity and Siri work comes from
  the other developer's round (`989f4c4`, `92c763f`, `7203f09`).
- Scope:
  - Shopping trip as a Live Activity: «Я в магазине» in the cart's progress header puts the cart
    on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with progress, the first three lines to buy
    with a check control, and a stop control (REQ-WIDGET-040…060, proposed; wording awaits
    owner approval).
  - Siri and Shortcuts: add items, read what is left, and start the trip by voice, in English,
    Russian and Ukrainian (REQ-SIRI-010…040, proposed; wording awaits owner approval).
  - The owner confirmed, relayed from the SDLC Orchestrator session on 2026-09-23, that the Live
    Activity and Siri work on `main` is final for 1.7.0. That does not approve REQ-WIDGET-040…060
    or REQ-SIRI-010…040, which stay proposed. A review of that work against them is part of this
    round, and the owner holds `tf-1.7.0-1` until its fixes land.
  - History day detail shows who added each item next to who bought it (REQ-SYNC-020); new
    optional attribute `HistoryItem.createdByName`, CloudKit field
    `CD_HistoryItem.CD_createdByName`.
  - Invite share sheet is presented from the Share cart row (a popover on a wide scene, the
    same sheet as before on a normal iPhone); the launch ride is centred on its own view with no
    fixed device size — preparation for iPhone Duo.
  - On-device diagnostics: MetricKit metric and diagnostic reports are written to the unified
    log (category `metrics`) through `MetricManager`; nothing leaves the device.
  - Engineering: a UI smoke suite (`OneCartUITests`, demo mode) runs in every `Tests` run;
    CloudKit participant decisions are unit-tested through a seam; Sign in with Apple and the
    repository's view-context reads are main-actor isolated (FU13).
- Release prerequisite: `CD_createdByName` on `CD_HistoryItem` deployed to the CloudKit
  Production schema — done 2026-09-23 (Console: "The schema is deployed to Production";
  the Production record type lists `CD_createdByName STRING`).
- Not verified by the agent: iPhone Duo (no installed simulator runtime supports the device),
  the Live Activity and Siri on a device, "added by" across two devices, and real MetricKit
  reports (the simulator delivers none).

## What’s New — English

- Going shopping? Start a shopping trip from the cart: your list appears on the Lock Screen and
  in the Dynamic Island, and you can check items off right there.
- Siri can add items to your cart, tell you what is left to buy, and start the shopping trip.
- History now shows who added each item as well as who bought it.

## What’s New — Russian

- Идёте в магазин? Начните поход прямо из корзины: список появится на экране блокировки и в
  Dynamic Island, и покупки можно отмечать прямо там.
- Siri может добавить товары в корзину, рассказать, что осталось купить, и начать поход в
