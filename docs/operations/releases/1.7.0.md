# OneCart Family 1.7.0

## Summary

- Version **1.7.0**, iPhone only (app and widget extension). Feature release after
  [1.6.0](1.6.0.md).
- Shopping trip as a Live Activity: «Я в магазине» in the cart's progress header puts the cart
  on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with progress, the first three lines to buy
  with a check control, and a stop control (REQ-WIDGET-040…060).
- Siri and Shortcuts: add items, read what is left, and start the trip by voice, in English,
  Russian and Ukrainian (REQ-SIRI-010…040).
- History day detail shows who added each item next to who bought it (REQ-SYNC-020); new
  optional attribute `HistoryItem.createdByName`, CloudKit field
  `CD_HistoryItem.CD_createdByName`.
- Invite share sheet is presented from the Share cart row (a popover on a wide scene, the
  same sheet as before on a normal iPhone); the launch ride is centred on its own view with no
  fixed device size.
- Live Activity behavior: Stop dismisses a finished trip at once; sign-out, deletion or a
  revoked login end a trip even while it is starting, and a failed startup keeps it; a
  relaunch adopts only the trip of the signed-in account and cart; a Lock Screen check updates
  the card before returning; a swiped-away card is noticed; the card goes stale an hour after
  its last change; refusals name their cause; trip progress is labelled for VoiceOver, the
  stop control is 44 × 44 pt and the check control 44 × 30 pt (three rows within the 160 pt
  card height).
- Siri behavior: a failed start is reported and retried instead of "sign in first"; requests
  are answered within 10 s, wait for a cart still being set up, report a partial add, split
  dictated lists on the word for "and" (Russian, Ukrainian, English), read items in the cart
  screen's order, and speak in the request's language with plural-aware counts.
- On-device diagnostics: MetricKit metric and diagnostic reports are written to the unified
  log (category `metrics`) through `MetricManager`; nothing leaves the device.
- Engineering: a UI smoke suite (`OneCartUITests`, demo mode) runs in every `Tests` run;
  CloudKit participant decisions are unit-tested through a seam; Sign in with Apple and the
  repository's view-context reads are main-actor isolated.
- Support: Settings ends with a Support section whose row writes to support or reports a
  problem, offensive content or a cart member, with Copy address when no email app opens
  (REQ-SHELL-070; App Review Guideline 1.2).
- Release builds compile no demo launch switch: `DemoLaunchOptions` and every reader of it are
  DEBUG-only (App Review Guideline 2.5.19).
- Release prerequisite: `CD_createdByName` on `CD_HistoryItem` deployed to the CloudKit
  Production schema.
- Not in this release: G14 (schema V9, author identity in activity notifications), which
  stays on branch `g14-schema-v9` until its CloudKit Production schema deploy.
- Not covered by automated checks: the Live Activity on a Lock Screen and in the Dynamic
  Island, Siri by voice, a Lock Screen check on a cold-launched app, the stale dimming,
  "added by" across two devices, and real MetricKit reports (the simulator delivers none).

## What’s New — English

- Going shopping? Start a shopping trip from the cart: your list appears on the Lock Screen and
  in the Dynamic Island, and you can check items off right there.
- Siri can add items to your cart (say several at once: "milk, bread and eggs"), tell you what is
  left to buy, and start the shopping trip.
- History now shows who added each item as well as who bought it.
- Settings → Support: write to us, or report a problem.

## App Review notes (draft)

The text for App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes. The response time is the
maintainer's commitment; change it there and in the support page draft
([release.md](../release.md#support-page-draft)) together.

> AUTHENTICATION
> This app uses Sign in with Apple. There is no email/password account or demo password. On a review device signed into an Apple ID with iCloud enabled, tap Sign in with Apple and complete the system sheet.
>
> PRIVATE FAMILY CARTS
> A cart is private to the people in it. Sharing uses iCloud sharing (CloudKit `CKShare`): a member sends the cart's iCloud share link from Settings → Share cart, and only people who open that link and accept it in iCloud join the cart. The app has no public content, no profiles outside a cart, no search for other users and no messaging. Members contribute short text only: item names, the cart name and their own display name.
>
> SAFEGUARDS
> - The cart owner removes a member in Settings (swipe the member, then Remove) and closes the invite link with Settings → Revoke invite link, so nobody new can join.
> - A member leaves the cart with Settings → Leave cart.
> - Any member deletes an item still to buy by swiping it in Cart, then Delete.
> - Settings → Support → "Contact support or report a problem" offers an email to the developer, prefilled with the app version and build, or copies the support address. Anyone can ask a question there or report offensive content or a cart member; we reply within two business days.
>
> NEW IN 1.7.0
> - Shopping trip: Cart → "I'm shopping" shows the cart as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with progress, the first three items to buy with a check control, and an end control.
> - Siri and Shortcuts (App Shortcuts, English, Russian and Ukrainian): "Add to OneCart", "What's left in OneCart", "Start shopping in OneCart".
> - History: a day's detail shows who added each item next to who bought it.
> - Diagnostics: MetricKit metric and diagnostic reports are written to the device's own log only. Nothing leaves the device, and the app has no analytics or tracking service.
> - Settings → Support, described above.
>
> CORE FLOW
> Add an item by name in Cart. Mark it Completed to show shopping progress. Items completed on a previous calendar day move to read-only History when the app opens or returns to the foreground. Invite another person from Settings → Share cart using the system iCloud share sheet.
>
> ACCOUNT DELETION
> Open Settings → Account → Delete Account and confirm. Successful deletion removes the user's private CloudKit data, clears local app data and the Sign in with Apple session, and returns to Welcome. If the user owns the active shared cart, it is deleted for its members. If the user is a member, the user leaves that shared cart and the owner's cart remains.
