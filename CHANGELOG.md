# Changelog

User-visible changes to OneCart Family. Release notes for shipped versions live in
[docs/operations/releases/](docs/operations/releases/).

## [1.7.0]

### Added

- Settings → Support: write to support, or report a problem, offensive content or a cart member.
  Without an email app, the support address is copied instead.

### Fixed

- An owner can remove a family member who joined with a phone number.
- At the largest text sizes, item names in the cart wrap between words instead of inside them.
